Create, connect, and celebrate with a hands-on paint party. SIS brings the materials and guided instruction so your guests can enjoy a relaxed, meaningful creative experience.

Request party details

What's included

  • One pre-selected wood sign project
  • All paints, brushes, and supplies
  • Step-by-step guided instruction
  • Mobile setup and cleanup
  • Approximately 2 to 2.5 hour workshop

Booking details

  • Starting price: $45 per person
  • Minimum: 8 paid participants
  • Maximum: 20 participants; larger groups available upon request
  • Host paints free with 8 or more paid participants
  • Additional travel fees may apply outside the standard service area

Reservation and payment

  • A $50 non-refundable reservation fee secures your event date
  • The reservation fee is applied toward the event balance
  • Final guest count is due 7 days before your event
  • Final payment is due on or before the day of your workshop

Cancellation policy

  • All sales are final. No refunds.
  • If a guest cannot attend, their registration may be transferred to another participant with advance notice.
  • Events may be rescheduled based on availability when requested with sufficient notice.
