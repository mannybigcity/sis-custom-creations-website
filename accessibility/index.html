Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 17, 2026

SIS Custom Creations and FRESH Apparel & Design are committed to making our website, information, and services welcoming and usable for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

Our approach

We work toward recognized accessibility guidance, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), as we maintain and improve the site. Our ongoing efforts include clear page structure, readable color contrast, meaningful image descriptions, keyboard-friendly controls, responsive layouts, and plain-language content.

Need assistance?

If a page, form, image, document, or feature is difficult to use, we want to help. We can provide information or help complete a request through another reasonable method when possible.

Email siscustomcreationstx@gmail.com or use our contact section. Please tell us which page or feature caused difficulty, what you were trying to do, and the best way to reach you.

Feedback and improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We welcome respectful feedback and will use it to prioritize practical improvements. Some linked third-party services, such as Shopify or Etsy, are controlled by those providers, but we will still help customers find an accessible path to information or service when we can.

Our goal: remove barriers, respond with care, and make every customer feel welcome.
