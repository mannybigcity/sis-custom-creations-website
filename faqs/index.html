Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you create, celebrate, or order with SIS.

What does SIS Custom Creations offer?

SIS offers mobile creative experiences, paint parties, kids' events, splatter paint parties, school and group experiences, fundraisers, custom creations, personalized apparel, home decor, laser engraving, and the monthly Irresistible DIY Box.

What kinds of experiences can I book?

You can book creative experiences for birthdays, families, schools, churches, daycares, teams, fundraisers, and community groups. SIS brings the creative setup to you and helps shape an experience around your event.

How are experiences priced?

Party packages are listed on the individual experience pages. Schools, churches, daycares, large groups, and custom events are quoted based on the group size, project, location, and details of your event.

How far does SIS travel?

SIS Custom Creations is based in the Cypress, Texas area and serves surrounding Houston-area communities. Some experiences reference a standard service area around Cypress, and additional travel fees may apply outside the standard service area.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A non-refundable reservation fee or deposit may be required to hold your event date because SIS begins planning, preparing materials, and reserving the schedule after booking. Deposit and balance details are confirmed before payment.

What does the host need to provide?

Most mobile experiences need enough tables and chairs for guests, access to the setup area, and a space that works for the selected project. SIS will confirm any special setup needs, drying space, water access, or protective-surface needs before the event.

How far in advance should I book?

Book as early as possible, especially for weekends, schools, churches, larger groups, fundraisers, holidays, and custom-themed projects. If your date is close, still reach out and SIS will confirm what is available.

What custom creations can I order?

SIS creates personalized wood and leather pieces, home decor, custom shirts, hats, cups, group items, reunion and trip pieces, and more. Tell us what you have in mind and we will help choose the right direction.

How do I place a custom order or ask about an event?

Connect with SIS through Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok. Share what you need, how many, your event or needed-by date, and any ideas or inspiration. SIS will follow up with the next steps and a custom quote when needed.

How long will my custom order take?

Timing depends on the item, quantity, materials, personalization, and current schedule. Reach out as early as you can, especially for events or larger group orders, and include your needed-by date so SIS can confirm availability.

What is included in the Irresistible DIY Box?

The $38 monthly DIY Box includes a themed wood project, coordinated paints, a paintbrush, glue, the supplies needed to complete the project, and instructions. It is designed to make creating at home simple and fun.

How do I subscribe to the DIY Box?

The IrreSIStible DIY Box subscription is being prepared for launch. Until checkout is fully ready, use the DIY Box page to join the interest list, ask about current seasonal kits, or request group quantities.

Can SIS help with a fundraiser, school, church, or large group?

Yes. SIS offers custom options for schools, churches, daycares, in-house events, fundraisers, and larger gatherings. Contact SIS with your group size and ideas so we can create the right plan.
